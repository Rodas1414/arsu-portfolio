Project 04

University
Landscape

Landscape Architecture Institutional 3D Visualization

Overview

Project Type Landscape Architecture
Category Institutional · Outdoor · Landscape
Tools Used AutoCAD · D5 Render · Photoshop · Illustrator
Designer Arsema Gebreselama

Outdoor spaces designed for learning, movement, and environmental connection.

The University Landscape project addresses the design of outdoor spaces within an institutional educational setting. The design premise recognizes that the spaces between buildings are as pedagogically significant as the buildings themselves — shaping how students move, gather, rest, and engage with their natural environment.

The landscape design establishes a layered network of pathways, gathering nodes, planted zones, and open expanses — each with a distinct spatial character and programme. Shade, seating, planting, and paving are coordinated to create an outdoor campus environment that is both functionally coherent and experientially rich.

3D visualization communicates the spatial sequence and vegetation strategy across seasons, presenting the landscape at human scale and demonstrating how the design performs across different times of day and year.
