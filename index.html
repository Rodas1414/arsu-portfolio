Architecture Thesis

Bachelor of Architecture · Unity University · 2026

Revitalizing Vernacular Architecture as a Sustainable Model for Tigray Hidmo House Design

An in-depth research and design thesis exploring how the traditional Tigray Hidmo vernacular house typology can serve as a sustainable architectural model for contemporary design. The work examines spatial organization, passive climate strategies, local material systems, and cultural continuity — proposing a framework that honors heritage while addressing present-day needs.

About

"Architecture, to me, is more than creating buildings — it is about shaping experiences, responding thoughtfully to people and place, and communicating ideas through space."

I am a creative and detail-oriented Architecture graduate with a deep passion for architectural and interior design. My work spans concept development, spatial solutions, technical drawing, and 3D visualization — bringing together a broad skill set to realize cohesive, considered design outcomes.

Currently based in Dubai, UAE, I am eager to contribute to dynamic design studios and practices that value precision, creativity, and meaningful design intent. I bring experience with industry-standard tools and a commitment to thoughtful, communicative design at every stage of the process.
